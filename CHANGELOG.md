## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-18

The first MVP.

### Added

- **Kanban board:** five columns, drag and drop, estimates agreed with the lead, lead review, reward on
  confirmation exactly once, reversals, idempotent writes, Work / Game switch.
- **Work layer:** reference-style cards, review queue with a stamp and “Undo”, new home and progress screens,
  dark theme and mobile tabs.
- **Co-op against the backlog:** team fortress, task horde, weekly waves with crowns, shared wave reward,
  overdue damage, progress instead of a leaderboard; friendly battles instead of raids.
- **3D game layer (three.js):** fortress, wave chest, city with construction scaffolding, troops, friendly
  battle replays on the opponent's city.
- **Challenges:** direct and open challenges to friendly battles, tavern.
- **City planning:** 16×16 island, land by town hall level, moving buildings, decorations.
- **Economy:** builder's hut, market with a fee, crystal well.
- **Army and defense:** archer and archery range, workshop with troop levels, mortar, traps, rider and stable,
  crossbow tower, town hall 6–7.
- **Team banner:** reinforcements from colleagues, garrison in friendly battles and fortress defense,
  decorations for waves.
